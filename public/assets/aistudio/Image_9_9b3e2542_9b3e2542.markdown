## Brand & Style

This design system translates the bold, theatrical energy of "BOATS THE MUSICAL" into an ethereal, high-culture digital storefront. The aesthetic blends **avant-garde editorial minimalism** with **sculptural nautical modernism**—drawing immediate inspiration from printmaking, linocut woodblocks, and monumental maritime silhouettes found in stage scenography.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Resonance
- **Luminous & Dramatic:** High-key, pristine white spaces punctuated by sheer blocks of electric ultramarine evoke the dramatic clash of stage lighting on open water.
- **Poetic & Avant-Garde:** Cultured, literary, and evocative, avoiding predictable tourist-theater tropes in favor of gallery-grade editorial poise.
- **Effortless Precision:** Premium ticketing flows, real-time seat selection, and curated theatrical merchandise presented with razor-sharp typography and intuitive spatial cues.

### Visual Style
The system pairs architectural graphic borders and maritime curves with airy whitespace. Contrast is crisp and purposeful: clean vector lines, subtle paper-grain textures, and precise geometric contours frame the stage experience.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by the vivid, deep saturation of theatrical cobalt/ultramarine balanced against breathable, bright canvas tints.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary (`#1D2B96` - Ultramarine Stage):** The dominant theatrical voice. Used for key action triggers, master navigation bars, high-impact titles, selected seat indicators, and dynamic linework.
- **Secondary (`#C99738` - Maritime Amber/VIP):** Reserved for VIP tier privileges, premier seat selection, exclusive limited merchandise badges, and gilded theatrical highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#2A7C8C` - Ocean Mist Teal):** Applied to standard ticket categories, informational notices, live stage status indicators, and secondary navigation elements.
- **Neutral Light Canvas (`#FFFFFF` to `#F4F6FC`):** A high-luminosity backdrop that gives typography and merchandise imagery breathing room.
- **Neutral Dark (`#0E143B` - Deep Abyssal Blue):** Utilized for primary text rendering, stage backdrop headers, and extreme-contrast borders, maintaining an ink-like richness rather than dull gray.

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional dialogue between the classical gravitas of European stage theater and the crisp, modern discipline of technical interfaces.

- **Headlines (Bodoni Moda):** High-contrast, editorial serif with dramatic vertical stress. Ideal for hero production billings, act announcements, and musical numbers. Titles feature generous leading to let character serifs stand out unhindered.
- **Body & Captions (Hanken Grotesk):** A sharp, contemporary neo-grotesque ensuring complete legibility across ticket manifests, cast bios, and merchandise specifications.
- **Labels, Dates & Seating Identifiers (Space Grotesk):** Technical geometric mono-influence providing structure for performance times, seat coordinates (e.g., `ORCH-A14`), barcode numbers, and monetary values. Always rendered with slight positive tracking in uppercase for immediate scannability.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout follows a disciplined 12-column responsive grid on desktop and 4 columns on mobile, framed like a classic theatrical stage bill.

### Structural Framework
- **Stage Proscenium Margins:** Outer page margins are intentionally wider (`margin: 3rem`) on desktop to simulate a framed stage opening.
- **Rhythmic Vertical Cadence:** Vertical spacing follows strict intervals of `space-md` (16px) and `space-xl` (48px) to distinguish production acts, ticketing steps, and product bundles.
- **Interactive Seating Canvas:** The theater seating chart utilizes an independent contextual panning frame with sticky bottom drawers for checkout summaries on mobile.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects muddy drop shadows, drawing instead on pure tonal layering, structural borders, and delicate cobalt ambient glow.

- **Level 0 (Stage Floor):** Crisp, pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or subtle mist tint (`#F8F9FD`).
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Surfaces):** Elevated through crisp 1px borders in `rgba(29, 43, 150, 0.12)` accompanied by an ethereal, diffuse tint: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(29, 43, 150, 0.06)`.
- **Level 2 (Modals & Sticky Cart Trays):** High-clarity frosted glass backdrop (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) with pure white opacity (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`), framed by a precise 1.5px cobalt keyline.
- **Level 3 (Selected Seat / Focus States):** Vibrant cobalt highlight with concentrated ambient projection: `0 0 16px rgba(29, 43, 150, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a refined, tailored radius level (0.25rem base) balancing crisp print edges with architectural grace.

- **Containers & Stage Cards:** Subtle 4px radius (`rounded`) preserving the geometry of playbills and physical theater tickets.
- **Notched Maritime Motifs:** Featured cards and ticket stubs can incorporate inward 8px corner cutouts or subtle bottom arch cutaways evocative of ship bow hulls.
- **Pills for Functional Tags Only:** Status indicators (e.g., `SELLING FAST`, `VIP ACCESS`) utilize full pill radii to stand out from rectangular content boxes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Select Seats", "Book Now"):** Solid Ultramarine background (`#1D2B96`), crisp white typography in `label-lg`, 4px radius, padded with `1rem 2rem`. On hover, subtle elevation with a golden accent bottom edge (`#C99738`).
- **Secondary ("Explore Cast", "View Merch"):** Pure white surface, 1.5px solid border in `#1D2B96`, text colored in `#1D2B96`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Text-only in `label-lg` with an animated wave underline on hover.

  - *Available Standard:* `#EBF3F5` fill with `#2A7C8C` border.

  - *Occupied:* Pale neutral gray (`#E5E7EB`) with faint diagonal slash, disabled interactions.
